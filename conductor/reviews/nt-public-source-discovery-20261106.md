midwives, Aboriginal health practitioners and medical officers, alongside a

coverage for a particular position or complete coverage of the health sector.

The source search exposed official indexed text but did not obtain and hash the
original agreement bytes. A direct FWC approved-agreement URL attempted for the
Aboriginal health practitioner instrument also returned HTTP 403. The indexed
text and titles are discovery evidence, not verified holdings, exhaustive
variation searches or accepted terms. A nominal expiry date does not by itself
establish cessation or replacement. Later versions, operative pay periods,
undertakings, covered employers and individual classification must be checked
against authoritative retained source editions before extraction or pricing.
The 2022 workforce workbook contains no NT-priced rows; no NT baseline is
inferred from another jurisdiction.
