Service. Its general commencement, exceptions for specified clauses, decision

[Western Health's approved Victorian copy](https://westernhealth.org.au/sites/default/files/2025-08/Approved%20Nurses%20and%20Midwives%20Agreement%202024-2028.pdf)
includes the FWC decision AE526693/PR781073. Definitions and clause 5 bind the
specified employee groups and Appendix 1 employers. Both RN and EN definitions
exclude employment solely/predominantly in public mental-health services; the
relevant service/department/unit/program matters, not simply individual duties.

role. These details prevent a universal registration/job-title gate. The approval
records NES priority for identified inconsistencies. Exact employer changes,
mental-health successor instruments and later variations remain separate work.

[SAET's order and agreement](https://www.saet.sa.gov.au/app/uploads/2022/12/Orders-and-EA.pdf)

Current interim administrative-pay authority and any successor are unqualified;

[WA Health's agreement library](https://www.health.wa.gov.au/Articles/A_E/Awards-and-Agreements)

and union eligibility and expressly excludes other enrolled-nursing streams,
