## Practitioner label versus general-employee salary values

The medical-scientist row 23 labels its grade `HP2-3`; the bioinformatics row
25 leaves the grade blank. Both cite the General Employees award and use annual
salary endpoints $69,031 and $139,576. The [1 March 2023 General Employees
award reprint](https://www.qirc.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-03/hospital_health_010323.pdf)
lists those values as professional-stream Level 2 pay point 1 and Level 6 pay
point 4 (PDF pages 18–19). The table says its annual amounts are reference
values calculated from rounded fortnightly rates and include the September 2022
wage ruling. The reprint's operative date must not be collapsed into the wage
ruling date.

For comparison, the separately held [Health Practitioners and Dental Officers
certified agreement](https://www.qirc.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-06/2023_cb43.pdf)
Schedule 1 (PDF page 55) lists HP2.1 at $69,387 and HP3.8 at $111,858 for
rates payable from 17 October 2022; its pay-point footnotes also limit particular
eligibility. Those figures and the General Employees schedule are different
industrial instruments and effective-date statements. The General Employees
amounts match the workbook numerically, but they do not establish that an
`HP2-3` employee is on its professional Level 2–6 scale, nor prove which
classification or source the workbook author intended. Both rows remain
unaccepted candidates pending duty, employer, instrument and pay-point review.

## Medical-officer classification range

The workbook's Queensland pathologist row 26 cites the Medical Officers award,
labels its classification range `L18-L27`, and records annual endpoints
$189,406 and $262,090. The [Medical Officers award reprint operative 1 March
2023](https://www.qirc.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-03/medical_officers_010323.pdf)
shows $189,406 at L18, $239,027 at L27, and $262,090 at L29 (PDF page 20,
visually inspected). Thus the upper workbook amount matches L29 in this table,
not the L27 described in its grade label. The reprint says its annual figures
are reference amounts based on rounded fortnightly rates and include a
1 September 2022 wage ruling. Reprint operation, wage-ruling date and any
certified-agreement or individual pay-effective dates are distinct.

This is an exact table comparison, not proof of author intent, a particular
pathologist's appointment, or the applicable pay agreement. Preserve the
original value and grade label as a conflicting historical candidate. A private
structured record retains the source hash, page, source dates and workbook
coordinates; redistribution rights remain unassessed.
