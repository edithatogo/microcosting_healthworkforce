correction: the downloaded file at the Attorney-General's 2022-named URL is
visibly labelled `SMOEA 2025` and must not be treated as the 2022 edition. Its
private hash `8df168f4554b8bd047063f268b9c3b469af46f415d49f383fdfef7a8bf9ef05f`
is retained to identify the actual bytes. The tribunal's [dated 2022 signed
agreement](https://www.saet.sa.gov.au/app/uploads/2022/02/ET-22-00216-Orders-and-signed-EA.pdf)

`13912dc0b0198c72e8a91420ca058abac4dd547a608b220b0417ecea65b8c530`.
Its scanned PDF pages 44 and 47 were rendered and visually inspected.

$292,019. In the 2022 agreement's Schedule 1, for employees with access to the
general public-sector salary-sacrifice scheme, consultant step 1 and senior
consultant step 9 are $220,914 and $291,086 from the first full pay period

exact. Schedule 2 has separate amounts for employees with access to the medical

date. This distinction matters to salary and on-cost interpretation; do not
combine the schedules or assume which scheme applies. The row's codes, actual
source, classification, employer, pay period and any other remuneration terms
remain to be established; no correction or employee entitlement is inferred.
