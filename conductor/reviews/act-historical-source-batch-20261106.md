## Historical coverage and candidate linkage

The administrative, support, technical and ambulance editions each use clause
A3 and Annex A; the medical edition uses clause 3 and Annex A, and the nursing
edition uses clause 3 and Schedule 1. The coverage clauses refer to the ACT
head of service, the then-named Calvary entity, qualifying persons in listed
classifications and relevant territory authorities, with stated exclusions.
The clauses occur on physical PDF pages 23, 20, 21, 19, 17 and 14 respectively
for administrative, support, technical, ambulance, medical and nursing.
Actual employer, classification and dates must be checked against the specific
instrument. The historical Calvary wording does not establish a current
employment relationship.

The private successor `gap-screening-linked-act-historical-v3.csv` retains all
576 existing target rows and existing columns, and adds a separate historical
candidate column. Eleven links associate seven historical editions with ten
ACT public-sector profession-family targets, including four candidate links to
the separately reviewed health-professional edition. All seven held PDF hashes
were reverified; the source-linking code validated target and jurisdiction
identity. The new links are leads for further occupation and employer review,
not findings of coverage. The successor SHA-256 is
`1d167e5a999ec8ecc2f5e95fad099ba5b517b437614ca7e80949b4beb421bc39`.

The medical variation decision reports a corrected Young outreach amount of
$2,691 and describes an increase from $2,620 by 1.35%. A single multiplication
of those displayed numbers gives $2,655.37; two successive 1.35% increases give
approximately $2,691.21. This is a numerical comparison, not a finding about
the decision's meaning or an instruction to change the published amount. The
calculation history and individual eligibility need further source review.

The official [2017–2021 medical agreement](https://www.cmtedd.act.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0008/1691693/ACTPS-Medical-Practitioners-Enterprise-Agreement-2017-2021.pdf)
independently confirms the $2,620 Young figure in clause 46.6.5 and ties
subsequent adjustment to clause 55. The 2021–2022 edition's clause 28.2 lists
two 1.35% pay increases, triggered by first full pay periods on or after
1 December 2021 and 1 June 2022, and clause 56.2 describes allowance
adjustments by the same percentages and dates. Applying two 1.35% increases to
$2,620 gives $2,691.217495 before rounding, which is close to the decision's
$2,691. This is a plausible arithmetic lineage, not proof of the applicable
starting amount, timing, rounding or intended interpretation. The decision's
stated amount and operative variation date remain authoritative source facts;
no individual entitlement or backpay is inferred.
