# Retention assessment draft

This module is under development and has not passed complete release qualification.
Do not use it to establish actual payroll entitlement or cash settlements.

The general anniversary path keeps snapshot FTE distinct from dated continuity,
location and explicitly measured source-week absences. Complete histories must
cover one resource without gaps or overlaps. Source-priced composition retains
authority, history, evidence and the derived assessment. Evidence reviews are
caller assertions; the library does not inspect referenced evidence files.

The separate pharmacy helper uses an explicitly reviewed credited-service/FTE
allocation convention and denominator. It does not infer calendar/workday units,
resolve changing-FTE policy, establish continuity or decide unpaid-leave credit.
Compensation and nonemployment cannot receive service credit. Pharmacy dated
source/receipt composition remains unfinished. No general four-week eligibility
rule is imported into pharmacy proration.

Still required: complete pharmacy binding, additional source-specific/adversarial
cases, full tests, installed-package checks, privacy review and hosted gates.
