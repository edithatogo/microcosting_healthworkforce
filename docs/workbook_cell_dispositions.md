# Candidate cell dispositions

Populated identity, source-metadata and measure-label columns receive explicit
meanings in the pinned historical workbook profile. Personnel/jurisdiction order
differs between input tables and long DSA tables; both layouts remain explicit.
Instrument aliases, reported grades and citations are unverified references.
Distribution descriptions stay notes rather than validated PSA priors. Reported
pay descriptions remain unparsed text with no numeric unit, despite the reused
weekly-pay heading. Existing base-year definitions remain unchanged; newly
classified DSA years are historical labels, not legal operative dates.

Six standalone currency/percentage labels on otherwise empty inline rows are
headers rather than additional jurisdictions. Medicare bucket/measure labels
stay separate from numeric count or ratio columns.

A section having column definitions does not prove every populated cell has a
disposition. Qualification reconciles the pinned cell inventory and checks every
nonheader cell for an explicit role, preserving source types, values, formulas,
absent caches, coordinates and original bytes. Candidate interpretations do not
resolve archived unknowns, scientific intervals/weights, source applicability
or accepted costing releases.
