# Candidate source links

`link_held_candidates` attaches explicit, reasoned associations between a
screening target and a held source edition in a validated `SourceRegistry`.
It checks target identity, source identity, held status, jurisdiction and
duplicate links. Existing discovery leads and candidate links are retained.

These associations do not establish occupation, sector or employer coverage,
worker eligibility, operative dates or industrial precedence. Every linked gap
remains `applicability_unassessed`; source bytes and their hashes must be
verified independently before relying on a holding assertion. A candidate link
does not promote a `SourceRecord` or create an accepted condition release.

## Queensland nursing vehicle costs

For excess private-vehicle travel, use EB12 clause 22 to establish the
qualifying journey and calculate the distance beyond the usual commute.
Use award clause 13.10 to select the dated rate for the vehicle type.
The September 2026 reprint lists AUD 1.00/km for motor vehicles and
AUD 0.34/km for motorcycles. The August reprint lists AUD 0.99/km and
AUD 0.34/km respectively. Retain the quote edition and journey evidence.

- [EB12 certified agreement](https://www.qirc.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-10/2025_cb126.pdf)
- [Award reprint and variation history](https://www.qirc.qld.gov.au/documents/3416)

For recurring executive nursing remuneration, C24 links EDON grade 11
band 4 and grade 12 to the SES 1 and SES 2 vehicle allowances. Verify
the policy and SES schedule for the appointment and valuation date.
Track this recurring allowance separately from authorised travel, tolls
and time off in lieu.

- [Queensland Health policy C24](https://www.health.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0026/164078/qh-pol-176.pdf)
